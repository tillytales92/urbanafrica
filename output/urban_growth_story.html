Bubble size is population. Position never rescales — only the dots move — so a city sliding right and down is spreading out faster than it's filling in; one sliding up is densifying.

Sources. GHSL BUILT-S & GHS-POP R2023A (European Commission JRC), eight epochs 1990–2025. Boundaries: Africapolis 2020 (OECD/SWAC), fixed at their 2020 vintage.

Caveat. A handful of cities had already sprawled past their mapped boundary by 1990 (Kinshasa, Luanda) — their land growth here is a floor, not a ceiling. Several small secondary cities' large % moves are partly polygon artifacts, not pure growth.
